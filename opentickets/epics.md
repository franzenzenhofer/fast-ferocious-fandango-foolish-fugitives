# JIRA Epics

## Context
- Product: Fast Ferocious Fandango: Foolish Fugitives (arcade car-ramming chase)
- Platform: Web (Vite), keyboard controls; mobile-first intent
- Primary flows: launch run, take control, ram traffic, manage heat/integrity, survive BUSTED loop
- Assumptions: no start menu, no audio, keyboard-only hints; demo mode starts on load

## Severity Scale
- S1: Blocker
- S2: Major
- S3: Minor
- S4: Polish

## Epics

### EPIC-001: Instant control clarity for first-time players
- Outcome: players understand how to take control and drive within 5 seconds
- Rationale: unclear controls undermine the "fun-to-play" first impression
- Impacted personas: P1, P2
- Linked tickets: TCK-001, TCK-002, TCK-003

### EPIC-002: Impact feedback that feels rewarding and readable
- Outcome: rams feel satisfying, with clear feedback on hit strength and damage
- Rationale: feedback drives fun and teaches skillful ramming
- Impacted personas: P1, P3
- Linked tickets: TCK-004

### EPIC-003: Survival pacing communicates danger and recovery
- Outcome: players anticipate BUSTED risk and understand recovery windows
- Rationale: surprise damage spikes feel unfair and reduce replay desire
- Impacted personas: P1, P2
- Linked tickets: TCK-005
